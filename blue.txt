**WEEK 12**

**Monday:**
I assisted in  the studio for  by checking and arranging the studio cameras, microphones, lighting equipment, monitors and cables. I observed how the equipment was positioned and connected before use.

**Tuesday:**
I observed the adjustment of studio cameras for proper picture quality. Attention was given to brightness, contrast, colour and framing to ensure that the camera produced a suitable picture for broadcast.

**Wednesday:**
I participated in testing microphone cables for continuity. The purpose of the test was to confirm that the cables were properly connected and capable of carrying the audio signal without interruption.

**Thursday:**
I observed the troubleshooting of a faulty connection in the studio. The technical officer checked the power source, cable, connector and equipment input systematically to identify the cause of the problem.

**Friday:**
I assisted in arranging tools and technical equipment after use. I also reviewed electrical and electronic safety precautions with the technical staff, particularly the need to handle cables, connections and powered equipment carefully.

**WEEK 13**

**Monday:**
I observed the complete broadcasting process from the studio through the control room and finally to the transmitter. This helped me understand how audio and video signals are processed and transmitted for broadcasting.

**Tuesday:**
I assisted in checking studio equipment before transmission. Cameras, microphones, monitors, cables and other relevant equipment were inspected to ensure that they were properly connected and ready for use.

**Wednesday:**
I monitored the transmitted signal and observed adjustments made to maintain good sound and picture quality. I learned how technical staff monitor both audio and video during transmission.

**Thursday:**
I participated in routine maintenance and cable management. Cables were properly arranged and labelled where necessary, while the technical area was kept neat and organized to reduce the possibility of connection problems.

**Friday:**
I reviewed the practical experience gained during the SIWES training and updated my logbook. I also discussed with the technical staff how the activities carried out during the training were related to electricity, electronics, sound waves, optics and electromagnetic waves.
